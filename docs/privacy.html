Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

DOGSAPKA AI Clipper ("the App") is a desktop application that runs entirely on the user's own computer, with an optional companion iPhone app that controls it over the user's local network. This policy explains what data the App handles. The App is provided by DOGSAPKA ("we", "us").

1. Data accessed from TikTok

When you connect your TikTok account, the App uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API with these permissions:

  • user.info.basic - to read your TikTok display name, so the App can show which account is connected.
  • video.upload - to send videos you choose to your TikTok inbox, where you finish posting them.

We do not access your followers, messages, analytics or any other TikTok data.

2. Data accessed from Google / YouTube and Twitch

When you connect a YouTube channel, the App uses the YouTube Data API to read the channel name and to upload the videos you choose. To monitor livestreams the App reads public stream status from the Twitch and YouTube APIs and, if enabled, public live chat to detect exciting moments.

3. Where data is stored

All data stays on your own devices. Access tokens are stored encrypted in the operating system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager / DPAPI, macOS Keychain). Clips, settings and account names are stored in a local database in a folder you choose. We do not operate any server, and no data is sent to us.

4. Data sharing

Data is only sent to the platform you choose to upload to (for example TikTok or YouTube), and only when a video is uploaded. We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Livestream chat

If chat-based highlight detection is enabled, the App reads public livestream chat. Usernames are immediately converted into anonymous, non-reversible codes, and chat messages are not saved.

6. iPhone remote control

The optional iPhone app talks only to the DOGSAPKA desktop app on your own network after you pair it with a one-time code. It stores the connection address and a secret access token in the iPhone Keychain. You can revoke a paired phone at any time in the desktop app.

7. Your choices and data deletion

You can disconnect any account at any time in the App (Settings → Publishing → Remove), which deletes the stored token from your computer. You can also revoke the App's access in your TikTok or Google account settings. Uninstalling the App and deleting its data folder removes all locally stored data.

8. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18.
